Guest notes

A few things to know

Venue comfort

Church & reception

The church has no air-conditioning and can be windy; it is often comfortably cool, so light, breathable fabrics are ideal. We kindly ask that only quiet, handheld (manual) fans be used during the ceremony — please refrain from electric fans, as louder noise may disturb the vows. The reception is in an event hall, so you need not worry about outdoor weather. If you feel cool at the resort, you are welcome to bring a light jacket for your own comfort.

Resort reminder

Event hall only

Kindly note that swimming and use of the resort pools are not allowed during our celebration. Please remain with our gathering in the event hall so everyone can enjoy the evening together.

Gift guide

Your presence is enough

If you wish to give a gift, cash is preferred via the QR codes on your invitation. Physical gifts are also warmly welcome.

Guest list

An evening for grown-ups

With love and appreciation for our little ones, we’ve planned an adults-only celebration so everyone can relax and enjoy the evening.

Our gathering is reserved for guests named on the invitation. We kindly ask that no additional guests or +1s be brought, so we can welcome everyone with care. Thank you for understanding — we can’t wait to celebrate with you.
